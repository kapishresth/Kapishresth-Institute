About Us

Kapishresth Institute is a premier educational institution located in Sector 18, Gurgaon, dedicated to providing quality education and fostering academic excellence. With experienced faculty and modern teaching methods, we prepare students for success in their professional careers.

Our mission is to empower students with knowledge, skills, and values that will enable them to become responsible citizens and successful professionals. We believe in holistic development and provide a conducive environment for learning and growth.

Located in the heart of Gurgaon's educational hub, Kapishresth Institute offers two prestigious graduation programs designed to meet the needs of students at different educational levels.

Admissions

Admission Requirements

📚 For Post 10th Graduation:

  • ✓ Completed 10th Standard Education
  • ✓ Minimum 40% marks in previous examination
  • ✓ Valid ID Proof (Aadhaar/PAN)
  • ✓ Character Certificate from previous school
  • ✓ Transfer Certificate

🎓 For Post 12th Graduation:

  • ✓ Completed 12th Standard Education
  • ✓ Minimum 45% marks in previous examination
  • ✓ Valid ID Proof (Aadhaar/PAN)
  • ✓ Character Certificate from previous school
  • ✓ Transfer Certificate

📋 Admission Process:

  1. Visit our campus in Sector 18, Gurgaon
  2. Fill the admission form
  3. Attend entrance assessment (if applicable)
  4. Document verification
  5. Fee payment & enrollment
